Products

  • ProtDeck Free profile (Galleon 100 SD, DA/EN) – one key = one action. Works with manual WoW keybinds, no addon required.
  • ProtDeck Keys addon (optional) – only sets your in-game keybinds to match the profile. You can bind manually instead.
  • ProtDeck Timers (planned, not released yet) – pull/countdown timers you start yourself. They read nothing from the game.

Quick fixes

  • A key does the wrong thing? With the optional addon, type /pdk conflicts. It lists your own keybinds that sit on deck keys and changes nothing. Without the addon, check Esc → Key Bindings for F13–F24.
  • Nothing happens in game? Run Stream Deck and WoW at the same permission level (both normal or both as admin), and use windowed fullscreen.
  • Dials: left = sound, right = pages (press = back to TANK). The screen text is static and never shows live cooldowns.
  • "Can't do that yet" after BUBBLE/BOP? That's Forbearance. PROTECT, BUBBLE, BOP, BOP ToT and DEBUFF OFF share one lockout.
  • Dansk: Forkert handling? Skriv /pdk conflicts (ændrer intet). Højre knap = sider, venstre = lyd. Forbearance-låsen deles af BESKYT, BOBLE, BOP, BOP ToT og DEBUFF AF.

Fair play

  • No automation: no multi-actions, no sequences, no keypresses sent by plugins.
  • No reading of game memory, pixels, screen, files or processes in any store product.
  • Personal experimental setups are not sold or supported here.
